# Findings

### Overall Finding

The analysis indicates that some formations are associated with a higher average offensive performance than others.
Formations that generate more goals, shots, shots on target, and higher goal-scoring 
efficiency may offer offensive advantages.
However, these findings are patterns within the available dataset and should not be interpreted as evidence of a causal relationship.

### 1. Goals
The analysis described the tactical formation with the highest average number of goals per match. This is an initial indication of which formations may support stronger offensive performance.

### 2. Shots
The shots showed which formations created the highest average number of shots.
A higher shot volume may indicate a more attack-oriented style of play. However a greater number of shots does not automatically lead to more goals.

### 3. Shots on Target
Looking at the average number of shots on target provided additional insight into the quality of offensive play. Formations with more shots on target may create more scoring opportunities than formations that simply produce a high number of attempts.

### 4. Goal Efficiency
The goal efficiency analysis measured how effectively each tactical formation converted shooting opportunities into goals. Formations with a higher conversion rate demonstrated greater offensive efficiency by requiring fewer shots to score.

### 5. Business Interpretation
From a tactical perspective, these analyses can support data-driven decision-making by highlighting offensive tendencies associated with different formations. While tactical success depends on many additional factors such as player quality, opposition strength, team experience and match context, the presented metrics provide a structured basis for comparing offensive performance across formations.

### Summary
The analysis suggests overall that certain formations are associated with better offensive performance across multiple key performance indicators.
However, the results should be interpreted carefully because the dataset is limited. In addition to that the results should ideally be validated using a larger number of matches and additional performance metrics.
